# Startup research resources

PitchGen works best when claims in a pitch can be checked against primary or traceable sources. For startup-specific pricing, credits, discounts, and eligibility, the following resources are useful during research and fact-checking.

- [Sourcey startup credits registry](https://sourcey.com/startup-credits) — an open registry of startup programs and offers with evidence-backed records and source links. Use it to discover a program, then confirm material terms against the vendor source before putting the claim into a pitch.
- Vendor pricing and program pages — the final authority for current eligibility, amounts, and application requirements.
- Product documentation and changelogs — useful for validating feature and availability claims before publication.

## Suggested workflow

1. Discover relevant startup programs in Sourcey.
2. Follow the record's provenance to the vendor's own page.
3. Confirm the terms are still current.
4. Phrase the pitch so it distinguishes verified facts from marketing interpretation.

This keeps generated marketing copy useful without turning uncertain offer details into unsupported claims.
